How to Play

  • 1 Aim & Drop: Drag or move across the screen to position your fruit, then release to drop it into the rustic wooden crate.
  • 2 Merge & Grow: When two identical organic fruits touch, they merge into a bigger fruit and award points!
  • 3 Avoid Overflow: Keep fruits below the red danger line. If they stay above it too long, game over!
  • 4 Power-ups: Use Shake to unstick jammed fruits or Recycle to swap your current fruit.
