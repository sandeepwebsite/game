How to Play

1. Word Chain Rule: Every word submitted must start with the exact last letter of the previous word (e.g., Apple ➔ Elephant ➔ Tiger).

2. Valid English Words: Only valid English dictionary words are accepted. Plurals, nouns, verbs, and adjectives are allowed.

3. No Repetition: Words cannot be reused once played in the current match.

4. Relaxed Pace: No countdown timer! Take your time to think of great words.

5. Multiplayer: In Online Friend mode, host or join with a 6-character room code using PeerJS P2P connection.
